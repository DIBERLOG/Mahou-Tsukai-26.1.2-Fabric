# Sound provenance

The eighteen family charge/release sounds in `sounds/cast/` and ninety-two individual scroll sounds in `sounds/spells/` are synthesized by `scripts/generate_spell_audio.py`. Build 0.30 added a distinct charge and release phrase for each of its 45 scrolls; 0.31 adds Fireball's two sounds for 46 scrolls in total. Fireball uses a warm harmonic ignition, a filtered noise roar and ember accents. The family sounds remain fallbacks for the staff and mana-capacity scrolls. The individual clips use synthesized timbres, distinct note phrases and spell-specific envelopes. They do not sample the film recording. The generator's `--only fireball` mode preserves all existing audio resources.

Starting with local build 0.29, the DOOM arrival and impact events use the same **War of the Worlds (2005)** excerpt, identified by its hosting page as “The noise the Alien Tripods make”:

- Source page: https://www.moviesoundclips.net/sound.php?id=79
- Downloaded Ogg: https://www.moviesoundclips.net/download.php?ft=ogg&id=1345
- Original duration: 9.264 seconds; mono Vorbis, 32 kHz.
- Original SHA-256: `1c7f876c26d7bb2cb376231d4685138ed7effcf3b509f952f21de70ff2b2b127`.
- Game resource: `assets/mahoutsukai/sounds/doom/tripod_film.ogg`.
- Processing: −4 dB gain, 25 ms fade-in, 80 ms fade-out, Vorbis re-encoding. Playback speed and pitch are unchanged.

This is a third-party film excerpt included at the user's explicit request. It is not an original synthesized asset, and no open redistribution license is claimed. Publishing this repository or a build does not grant a license to reuse the recording. The synthesis script preserves these externally sourced sound entries.

Starting with local build 0.32, DOOM adds two original synthesized layers, generated by this repository's own `scripts/generate_doom_audio.py`:

- `assets/mahoutsukai/sounds/doom/rumble.ogg` — low 24–48 Hz sub-bass with filtered noise and a slow breathing envelope, 4.0 s, mono Vorbis 32 kHz. Played as the low attraction rumble during the eight-second preparation.
- `assets/mahoutsukai/sounds/doom/impact.ogg` — a pitch-dropping sub hit, a filtered noise burst and a short metallic ring, 2.9 s, mono Vorbis 32 kHz. Layered under the film excerpt for the final strike.

Both clips are synthesized from the script's own sine partials, seeded noise and envelopes; they contain no samples from the film recording or any other third-party audio. They are original assets of this project. The generator's random seeds are fixed, but the Ogg stream serial number makes the encoded bytes differ between runs; SHA-256 of one generated pair: rumble `2b9bb20ab4663f8f19d95198e27f4c35bba41af9b37b281c32b6e13769f57594`, impact `2c55227298ce7105d55cbf59c2690bb5f99a669233285fab13bf0c5ba43e9129`.

Build 0.32 also adds one original synthesized layer for the Mystic Staff's big explosion mode: `assets/mahoutsukai/sounds/mystic/big_explosion.ogg` (1.9 s, bright crack plus a short sub drop, mono Vorbis 32 kHz), generated by the same script and registered as `mahoutsukai:mystic.explosion_big`. It is synthesized from the script's own noise and sine partials, contains no third-party samples, and is played once per blast instead of once per nearby player.


Build 0.33 fixes the mono encoder buffer: the intended 4.0/2.9/1.9 second clips are no longer duplicated into 8.0/5.8/3.8 seconds. The rumble repeats every 90 ticks; at pitch 0.92 its 4.35-second playback fits within the 4.5-second interval.

Build 0.34 adds three original Archmage clips in `sounds/archmage/`: charge (0.9s), release (0.45s), shockwave (1.3s), synthesized by `scripts/generate_archmage_audio.py` from seeded noise and sine/gong harmonics, mono Vorbis 32kHz. They contain no film samples. Native evoker voice and preparation sounds are reused from Minecraft.

Build 0.36 adds two original synthesized Archmage clips using the same generator: `arrival.ogg` (2.5 s, gold chord, rising atmosphere and bass pulse) and `defeat_laugh.ogg` (3.2 s, four low voiced/formant laugh pulses with falling pitch and quiet echoes). Both are mono Vorbis 32 kHz, use no external recordings, and play at pitch 1.0. The arrival starts after a nearby observer has loaded the dimension. The defeat laugh is sent privately to the defeated player after respawn has loaded, once per qualifying defeat. Independent `--only arrival` and `--only defeat_laugh` regeneration preserves the other clips.
